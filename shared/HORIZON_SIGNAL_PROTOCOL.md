# Horizon Signal Protocol — v0.6

Horizon research should identify credible future change, not generate news volume.

## 1. Signal Types
- enacted/adopted but not effective;
- formal legislative proposal;
- regulator proposed rule;
- draft guidance/consultation;
- regulator work programme;
- pending apex/high court case or appeal;
- announced enforcement priority;
- repeated enforcement pattern;
- official speech/statement indicating intended direction.

## 2. Signal Strength
Assess separately:
```yaml
adoption_probability: high | medium | low
legal_impact_if_adopted: high | medium | low
expected_timing: date_or_range
business_lead_time: long | medium | short
```

## 3. Priority
Priority is highest where:
- legal impact is high;
- adoption probability is high/medium;
- business implementation lead time exceeds likely legal lead time.

## 4. Event Lifecycle
Treat proposal → consultation → adoption → effective date → enforcement as one linked regulatory event chain, not five unrelated news items.

## 5. Current-Law Firewall
No horizon signal may alter the statement of current law unless and until its legal status permits. It may change risk appetite or preparation recommendations separately.
