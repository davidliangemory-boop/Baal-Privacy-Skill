# Analogical Fit Protocol — v0.6

Use for cases, enforcement decisions, FAQs, examples, and fact patterns relied on in Step 3.

## 1. Analogy Dimensions
Score or describe fit across:
- actor/controller type;
- data category/sensitivity;
- data subject relationship (employee, consumer, child, patient, supplier, etc.);
- processing purpose;
- processing operation/technology;
- scale/frequency/duration;
- lawful-basis structure;
- jurisdiction/statutory version;
- safeguards/transparency;
- harm/risk profile.

## 2. Fit Labels
- `DIRECT` — materially same legal and factual pattern.
- `STRONG_ANALOGY` — same rule and most dispositive facts.
- `LIMITED_ANALOGY` — useful only for one factor or proposition.
- `BACKGROUND_ONLY` — context; should not drive the conclusion.

## 3. Anti-Overfitting Rule
Do not infer a general prohibition/permission from a single enforcement decision unless the decision itself states a general rule or higher authority supports it.

## 4. Divergence Rule
If multiple analogous decisions point differently, identify the factual or legal pivot causing divergence before synthesising them.
